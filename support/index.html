Nightfeed support

Write to info@apexweb.hu and you will get a reply from the person who makes the app.

Questions

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Nightfeed. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then tap the area under the clock.

Why did my lines change? Lines follow your baby's age. When your baby moves into a new stage, the lines move with them. You can change the birthday or due date in the app under You.

How does the streak work? Tap Say it once a day, in the app or on the widget. One rest day a week keeps your streak. Two missed days in a row start a new one.

How do I cancel Nightfeed Plus? Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Nightfeed. If you cancel during the free trial, you pay nothing.

How do I restore my purchase? In the app, open You and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I delete my data? In the app, open You and tap Erase all my data. Everything is stored only on your iPhone.

When it's more than a hard day

Nightfeed is a few kind words a day. It is not medical care. If low or anxious feelings last more than two weeks, get in the way of looking after yourself or your baby, or you have thoughts of harming yourself, please tell someone today: your doctor, midwife, health visitor or someone you trust. If you are in danger right now, call your local emergency number.

United States: Postpartum Support International. United Kingdom: NHS, feeling depressed after childbirth.
