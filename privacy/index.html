Nightfeed privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Nightfeed is an iPhone app with short daily lines and widgets for new and expecting moms. It is operated by Tokár Ádám, sole proprietor, Hungary.

The short version

Nightfeed collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your baby's name (optional) and birthday or due date, the moments you picked, your streak, saved lines and the lines you write yourself are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its widgets.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • You can erase everything at any time in the app: You, Erase all my data. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Purchases

Nightfeed Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether Plus is active.

Children

Nightfeed is meant for adults. It does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
